How MALAI VIZHI Works

End-to-end predictive pipeline translating environmental metrics into actionable early warnings.

Scientific Processing & Warning Pipeline

01
DATA INGESTION

Meteorological & Satellite Feeds

NASA POWER API pulls daily PRECTOTCORR precipitation models, combined with ground telemetry from 86 remote rain gauges across the NER corridor.

↓
02
PHYSICAL SENSING

Soil Moisture & Saturation Modeling

Ground capacitive probes measure volumetric water content (VWC). Antecedent precipitation indices (API) compute the cumulative saturation of sub-surface soil layers.

↓
03
GEOSPATIAL ANALYSIS

Terrain & Slope Physics (DEM)

Digital Elevation Model data evaluates slope steepness (18°–45°), vegetative shear reinforcement, and historical fault plane vulnerability.

↓
04
AI INFERENCE

Multi-Parameter Risk Classifier

Trained XGBoost models output a normalized composite AI Risk Score (0–100) and classify danger into LOW (<50), MODERATE (50–74), and HIGH (75+).

↓
05
DISPATCH & RESPONSE

Disaster Authority & Citizen Alerts

Critical threshold breaches instantly generate geo-targeted early warnings, populating the command center and triggering community advisories hours before slope failure.

Platform Technical Architecture

Backend Core: Python 3.13 Flask + SQLite Microservices
Satellite Provider: NASA POWER Agroclimatology (PRECTOTCORR)
Geospatial Mapping: Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap Vector Grid
Risk Algorithm: Physics-Informed Pore Water Pressure Classifier
Hackathon Theme: Smart India Hackathon (SIH) — Disaster Management
Data Integrity: Strict separation of Satellite Ingestion vs Demo Simulation
